❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which PM tool has the best affiliate commission?
Motion offers 30% lifetime recurring — the highest. A single referral on $19/mo earns $6.84/month forever. ClickUp offers 15-30% lifetime ($1.05-2.10/mo per referral on $7/mo plan, plus $100 CPA for Enterprise). Notion and Todoist offer 20% recurring for 12 months.
What's the difference between Notion and ClickUp?
Notion is more flexible (databases, wikis, docs as building blocks) but has weaker PM features (no native Gantt, no time tracking, weaker dependencies). ClickUp is purpose-built for PM with 16+ views but has a steeper learning curve. Notion is better for knowledge management; ClickUp is better for project execution.
Which PM tool is best for a solopreneur on a tight budget?
Todoist Pro at $4/mo is the cheapest paid option. ClickUp's free tier handles unlimited tasks and is generous enough for serious solo work. Notion's free tier is unlimited for personal use and the most versatile.
Can I use these for content calendar / affiliate management?
Yes. Notion's database views let you track posts by keyword, affiliate link, platform, and status — perfect for content planning. ClickUp has a dedicated content calendar template. Todoist works well for simple content scheduling. Asana has a marketing calendar template.
Do these tools have good affiliate programs for creators?
Yes — ClickUp (15-30% lifetime), Motion (30% lifetime), Notion (20%/12mo), Linear (20%/12mo), and Todoist (20%/12mo) all have strong programs. Most use Rewardful or Impact. Lifetime recurring programs (ClickUp, Motion) are best long-term earners.
