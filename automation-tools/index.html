Frequently Asked Questions

Which automation tool has the best affiliate commission?
Zapier offers 30% recurring for 12 months — the highest rate. A single referral on a $29.99/mo plan earns ~$108/year. Make offers 20% recurring for 12 months. For volume, Zapier's brand recognition makes it easiest to convert referrals.
What's the difference between Zapier and Make?
Zapier has more integrations (7,000+) but costs more ($19.99/mo starter) and uses sequential 'Zaps'. Make has fewer integrations (2,000+) but offers a visual builder for complex multi-branch logic and starts at $9/mo.
Which automation tool is best for a solopreneur on a budget?
Make at $9/mo offers the best value. n8n self-hosted is free but requires technical setup. Activepieces self-hosted is also free.
Can I automate social media posting with these tools?
Yes. Make connects to Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and TikTok. Zapier has similar integrations. For content repurposing workflows (blog post → social posts → email), Make's visual builder is most intuitive.
Which tool is best for e-commerce automation?
Zapier has the most e-commerce integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Etsy, Amazon). Make is nearly as capable at half the price.
