Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 'recurring' and 'lifetime' commissions?
Lifetime recurring means you earn a commission every month as long as the referred customer remains a paying customer, with no end date.
Which affiliate network pays the fastest?
Most networks pay on net-30 or net-60 terms. Rewardful and FirstPromoter typically pay out every 30 days.
Should I prioritize high commission rate or long cookie window?
For comparison pages and affiliate content that ranks on Google, cookie window matters more than commission rate.
