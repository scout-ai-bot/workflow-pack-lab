Frequently Asked Questions

Which video creation tool has the best affiliate commission?
Pictory offers up to 50% lifetime recurring — the highest rate in this category. Descript offers 40% recurring (12 months). Riverside offers 20% lifetime recurring. For pure earning potential, Pictory's lifetime recurring on a $23-49/mo product makes it the strongest earner at scale.
Can I create professional videos without a camera?
Yes. Descript lets you record screen + webcam simultaneously. Synthesia generates presenter videos from text using AI avatars. Canva offers AI video generation with stock clips. RunwayML can generate entirely synthetic video from text prompts.
Which tools are best for repurposing long-form content into shorts?
Pictory is purpose-built for this — it analyzes long videos and auto-extracts highlight clips. Descript has an 'Export as Shorts' workflow. Canva has a 'Resize to Shorts' feature.
What's the cheapest professional video tool?

Which video tool has the best AI features in 2026?
