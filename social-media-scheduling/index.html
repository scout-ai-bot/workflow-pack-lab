❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which social media scheduler has the best free tier?
Buffer offers the most generous free tier — one calendar channel, 10 scheduled posts, and basic analytics permanently. Metricool's free tier also provides permanent access to analytics plus 10 posts. Later's free tier (1 social set, 10 posts/platform) is also strong for Instagram-focused creators.
What's the best scheduler for Twitter/X + LinkedIn text content?
Typefully is the clear winner for text-first platforms. It's purpose-built for X/Twitter and LinkedIn with AI thread drafting, tone rewriting, and engagement analytics. Buffer is the best alternative if you need visual platform scheduling alongside text content.
Which tool has the highest affiliate commission rate?
Publer offers 30% recurring (lifetime) — the highest rate in this comparison, translating to $3/mo per referral on their $10/mo plan. Typefully offers 25% recurring (lifetime). Buffer, Later, Metricool, and SocialBee all offer 20% recurring (lifetime). Hootsuite's 15-25% is only for 12 months.
Do these tools support AI content generation?
Yes — every tool in this comparison now includes AI features. Buffer's AI assistant handles caption generation and content ideas. Hootsuite's OwlyWriter is purpose-built for social copy. Later has AI caption writing. Publer, Metricool, SocialBee, and Typefully all include AI content generation in their standard plans.
Which scheduler is best for managing multiple client accounts?
Hootsuite is the industry standard for agency use with robust team collaboration features, approval workflows, and white-label reporting. Buffer's Essentials plan supports multiple channels per account. SocialBee's category system makes it easy to manage content for different brands.
