❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which VPN has the fastest speeds?
NordVPN consistently ranks #1 in independent speed tests thanks to its NordLynx (WireGuard-based) protocol. ExpressVPN's Lightway protocol is also very fast. Surfshark's WireGuard implementation is competitive. For streaming, ExpressVPN and NordVPN are the most reliable for unblocking services like Netflix, BBC iPlayer, and Disney+.
Which VPN pays the highest affiliate commission?
NordVPN pays up to 100% commission on 1-month plan signups, 40% on 1-year and 2-year plans, and 30% revenue share on renewals (30-day cookie). Surfshark and ExpressVPN use similar tiered structures — the "100%" headline rate applies to monthly plans only, not multi-year plans. PIA pays up to $65/sale with recurring options. ProtonVPN offers 20% recurring on all plans. Always verify current terms in the partner dashboard before relying on a rate.
Is a free VPN worth using?
Only ProtonVPN's free tier is genuinely recommended — it's ad-supported, moderately fast, and has a strong privacy policy (Swiss jurisdiction, no logs). Most free VPNs monetize by selling user data or serving invasive ads. For affiliate promotion, focus on paid VPNs — the commissions are higher and the products are better.
Which VPN is best for streaming and unblocking content?
ExpressVPN and NordVPN are the most reliable for streaming — they consistently unblock Netflix libraries from different countries, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Hulu, and Amazon Prime. ExpressVPN's MediaStreamer feature also allows VPN-like functionality on devices that don't natively support VPNs (smart TVs, game consoles).
Do any VPNs offer free trials?
Most premium VPNs offer 30-day money-back guarantees rather than free trials. Surfshark offers a 7-day free trial on mobile platforms. ProtonVPN has a genuinely usable free tier. For affiliate purposes, the 30-day guarantee is usually sufficient — users are comfortable committing with a refund safety net.
