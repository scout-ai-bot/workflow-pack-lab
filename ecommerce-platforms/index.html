📊 Detailed Platform Analysis

Shopify

Powering 4.8 million+ stores globally, Shopify is the undisputed market leader. The platform handles everything from product listing and inventory to payments, shipping, and multi-channel selling. Its app marketplace (6,000+ integrations) means you can extend virtually any functionality. The Shopify Affiliates program pays for every referred subscription — exact rates should be verified in your partner dashboard as they vary by region and tier.

WooCommerce

WooCommerce powers ~28% of all e-commerce stores (the largest share of any platform). Being open-source means zero lock-in and unlimited customization, but you'll need managed hosting ($3-20/mo via Hostinger/Bluehost/SiteGround) and may need paid extensions for advanced features. WooCommerce has no direct cash affiliate program, so monetization comes from hosting referrals — cross-reference our Web Hosting Comparison page.

BigCommerce

BigCommerce's key differentiator: zero transaction fees on every plan. Combined with built-in multi-channel selling (Amazon, eBay, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest), this makes it ideal for growing businesses that want to scale without platform fee erosion. The affiliate program via Impact offers strong terms: 75% of first month's revenue or $150 flat per referral, plus 10% recurring.

Squarespace

Best known for award-winning design templates, Squarespace's ecommerce plans (Commerce Basic $33/mo, Commerce Advanced $49/mo) include inventory management, CSV exports, and integrated shipping. No transaction fees on Commerce plans. The Squarespace Affiliates program (via Impact) pays 30% recurring for the first year — strong for their premium price point.

Wix

Wix's drag-and-drop builder with 800+ templates makes it beginner-friendly. The AI-powered site builder can generate a store design from a brief. Transaction fees start at 2.9% on Basic and drop to 2.4% on VIP. Wix Affiliates pays up to $100 per referred subscription — a flat-fee model that is simple to track.

Ecwid (by Lightspeed)

Ecwid's unique value is embeddable: add a full shopping cart to any existing website (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, or custom HTML) in minutes. Its free plan supports up to 5 products. Paid plans unlock 0% transaction fees, multi-channel selling, and advanced features. The 90-day cookie window is industry-leading — Ecwid affiliates at 20-30% recurring earn on a longer attribution window than most competitors.

Sellfy

Sellfy specializes in digital products and print-on-demand. Built-in PDF protection, print fulfillment (through Printful/Printify integration), and instant digital delivery make it ideal for creators. 0% transaction fees on Business and Premium plans. Sellfy's affiliate program via FirstPromoter pays 30% recurring with a 90-day cookie.

Frequently Asked Questions

Shopify vs WooCommerce — which is better for a new store?
Shopify if you want to launch fast with minimal technical setup — everything included out of the box. WooCommerce if you want full control, lower long-term costs, and better SEO. Shopify costs ~$348/year (Basic plan) plus transaction fees. WooCommerce costs ~$36-240/year for hosting plus extensions as needed. For a content-driven store focused on organic traffic, WooCommerce + good hosting often wins on cost and flexibility.
Which e-commerce platform has the lowest total cost?
WooCommerce has the lowest entry cost — the plugin is free and entry-level hosting starts at $2.69/mo (Hostinger). No transaction fees beyond payment processing. For a hosted solution, Ecwid's free plan supports up to 5 products with no monthly fee. Sellfy Starter ($19/mo) with 2% transaction fees is the next cheapest hosted option for digital products.
Which platform has the best affiliate program for me as a promoter?
BigCommerce offers the most straightforward terms: $150 flat per sale or 75% of first month regardless of plan tier. Ecwid and Sellfy offer lifetime recurring commissions with 90-day cookies — best for passive income on content that ranks long-term. Squarespace's 30% first-year recurring is strong for their premium price point. Wix's flat $100/referral is simple and predictable. Shopify's rates vary — verify in your dashboard.
Do these platforms charge transaction fees on top of payment processing?
No transaction fees: BigCommerce (all plans), Ecwid, WooCommerce, Sellfy (Business & Premium). Transaction fees apply: Shopify (2% extra if not using Shopify Payments), Squarespace (3% on Business plan), Wix (2.4-2.9% depending on plan), Sellfy (2% on Starter plan). These fees are ON TOP of standard payment processing (typically 2.9% + 30¢).
What's the best e-commerce platform for affiliate marketing?
WooCommerce is the most affiliate-friendly — tons of affiliate plugins (AffiliateWP, YITH, WooCommerce Affiliates), full control over commission structures, and best-in-class content SEO for organic affiliate traffic. Shopify also has strong affiliate apps through its marketplace but with less customization. For an affiliate site selling physical products, WooCommerce + AffiliateWP is the gold standard.
