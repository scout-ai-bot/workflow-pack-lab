❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which website builder has the best affiliate program?
For one-time high payouts: Wix and Squarespace (50% first payment, $50-200/sale). For recurring passive income: Webflow (30% for 12 months) and Framer (20% lifetime) and Carrd (50% lifetime). For volume: Hostinger (60% first payment at $2.99/mo converts easily). The best program depends on your audience — design-focused content performs better with Webflow/Framer, while general small business content converts better with Wix/Hostinger.
Which website builder pays the highest total commission?
Webflow has the highest total payout potential per referral. A single $28/mo CMS plan referral earns you ~$100 over 12 months (30% × $28 × 12). For agency referrals on $89/mo plans, that's ~$320/year per referral. Wix and Squarespace pay $50-200 upfront per referral — better for immediate cash flow. Framer and Carrd excel at long-tail passive income with lifetime recurring commissions.
Are there website builders with free plans that convert well for affiliates?
Yes — platforms with generous free plans (Wix, Carrd, Framer, Webflow) are excellent for affiliate marketing because users can try the product risk-free and upgrade naturally. Carrd is particularly effective: its free 3-site plan lets users build before upgrading to Pro ($9-19/yr), and the lifetime cookie means you get credited for upgrades even months later. Framer's free plan (1,000 visitors/month, .framer.website subdomain) is another strong converter for the startup/designer audience.
Which website builder is best for SEO?
Webflow offers the strongest SEO capabilities — clean semantic code, customizable meta tags, automatic sitemaps, canonical URLs, 301 redirects, and fast load times by default. Squarespace is second-best with built-in SEO features and clean code. Wix has improved significantly but still generates more code bloat. For affiliate content focused on SEO-savvy audiences, Webflow is the recommended pick.
What's the difference between one-time and recurring affiliate commissions?
One-time commissions (Wix, Squarespace) pay a percentage of the customer's first payment only — a larger immediate payout but no ongoing revenue. Recurring commissions (Webflow: 12 months; Framer: lifetime; Carrd: lifetime) pay a percentage of each renewal payment, building passive income over time. For autonomous revenue operations, recurring commissions are preferable because they create compounding income without additional effort. A portfolio of 100 Webflow referrals at $28/mo CMS plans = ~$840/month passive income after 12 months of renewals.
Can I promote multiple website builder affiliate programs?
Yes — and you should. A well-built comparison page naturally includes all major options, and visitors appreciate the transparency. Use affiliate disclosure (as we do on this page), present data honestly, and let visitors choose. Most affiliate programs only prohibit spamming or misrepresentation — honest comparison content is encouraged.
