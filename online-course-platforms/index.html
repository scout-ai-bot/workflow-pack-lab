❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which course platform has the lowest fees?
Thinkific and Podia offer 0% transaction fees on paid plans — you keep 100% of course revenue minus payment processing (2-3%). Teachable's Basic plan charges 5% but the free plan has 0% transaction fee. Kajabi is the most expensive monthly but also 0% transaction fees.
What's the best platform for affiliate commissions?
Kajabi offers the highest-value affiliate commissions — 30% lifetime recurring on $149-399/mo plans means $44-119/mo per referral, indefinitely. Podia and Teachable both offer 30% recurring. Thinkific offers 20% lifetime + $200 CPA for enterprise. Skillshare offers 30% on subscriptions.
Can I sell courses on multiple platforms at once?
Yes — many creators use a 'hub and spoke' model: Teachable or Podia as their primary storefront (keep 100% of direct sales), plus Udemy/Skillshare for organic discovery (accept revenue split for marketplace traffic). Most platform ToS allow multi-platform distribution as long as pricing is consistent.
Which platform has the best built-in marketing tools?
Kajabi has the most comprehensive marketing suite — email sequences, landing pages, funnels, pipelines, and affiliate management all built in. Teachable has solid marketing features including coupons, bundles, and affiliate management. Podia is simpler but includes email marketing and affiliate management in all plans.
Do I need to create video content for online courses?
Video is the most engaging format and preferred by students, but not strictly required. Many successful courses are text-based with downloadable worksheets, checklists, and templates. AI tools like Descript, Synthesia, and Canva make video creation accessible even without filming yourself.
