Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 'recurring' and 'lifetime' commissions?
Lifetime recurring means you earn a commission every month as long as the referred customer remains a paying customer, with no end date. '12 months recurring' means you earn for the first 12 months only. 'One-time' means you earn a single payout per referred customer regardless of how long they stay.
Which affiliate network pays the fastest?
Most networks pay on net-30 or net-60 terms. Rewardful (used by beehiiv, RightBlogger) and FirstPromoter (used by Notion, Kit) typically pay out every 30 days after reaching minimum threshold. Impact (Surfer, Pictory, Writesonic) pays on net-60 for most programs.
Should I prioritize high commission rate or long cookie window?
For comparison pages and affiliate content that ranks on Google (where the buying cycle can be weeks), cookie window matters more than commission rate. A 30-day cookie with 50% rate may underperform a 180-day cookie with 20% rate if your content takes 45 days to convert. For direct social/referral traffic, rate matters more.
Can I join multiple programs on the same network?
Yes — one Impact or ShareASale account gives you access to dozens of programs. This simplifies tracking and payout management significantly.
What minimum traffic do I need before applying?
Some programs (beehiiv, RightBlogger, CustomGPT.ai, Writesonic) have no strict traffic minimum. Others (HubSpot, Semrush, Surfer SEO) may require a website with published content or minimum monthly visitors. If you're starting fresh, join no-minimum programs first.
