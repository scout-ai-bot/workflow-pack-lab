Detailed Platform Breakdown

Impact — The Enterprise Standard

Used by: Hostinger, Semrush, HubSpot, Canva, Surfer SEO, Writesonic, Pictory, NordVPN, Surfshark

Impact is the largest affiliate network by merchant quality. If you're a creator looking to promote SaaS and hosting products, Impact handles the most high-ticket programs on this list. For merchants, it's expensive ($500+/mo) but provides full partnership automation, fraud detection, and global payment handling.

For affiliates (publishers): Free to join, $50 minimum payout, net-60 payment terms. Requires an application per program.

Rewardful — Best for SaaS Startups

Used by: beehiiv (50% for 12 months), RightBlogger (50% lifetime), Tally.so, CustomGPT.ai, Relay.app

Rewardful is the simplest way to launch an affiliate program for a SaaS product. It integrates natively with Stripe and Paddle — no developer work needed. It automatically tracks recurring commissions, coupon codes, and tiered rates. Starting at $49/mo with a 14-day free trial, it's the most accessible platform for new affiliate programs.

ShareASale — The Marketplace Heavyweight

Used by: Bluehost, Awin merchants, thousands of ecommerce and hosting brands

ShareASale has been around since 2000 and has one of the largest affiliate marketplaces. For merchants, the $550 one-time setup fee and 20% rev share is steep but gives access to thousands of active affiliate publishers. For affiliates, it's free with a $50 minimum payout and a huge catalog of programs across every niche.

PartnerStack — B2B Partnership Hub

Used by: NordVPN (via PartnerStack affiliate program), Surfshark, various B2B SaaS

PartnerStack focuses on B2B partnerships including affiliate, referral, and channel partner programs. It's especially strong for companies with multiple partnership types running simultaneously. Pricing is custom but typically $300+/month.

FirstPromoter — Affordable Recurring Tracking

Used by: Notion, Kit (ConvertKit), n8n, many mid-market SaaS products

FirstPromoter is Rewardful's closest competitor — similar pricing ($47/mo starting), similar integration depth (Stripe, Paddle, Zapier), and similar recurring commission capabilities. Its key differentiator is a lower $25 minimum payout for affiliates and a slightly more mature analytics dashboard.

Tapfiliate — Ecommerce & Course Friendly

Used by: Online course creators, ecommerce stores, digital product sellers

Tapfiliate is the best option if you're selling physical or digital products through Shopify or WooCommerce. It supports one-time commissions, recurring commissions, and multi-tier affiliate structures. Starting at $69/mo with a 14-day free trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an affiliate network and an affiliate management platform?
An affiliate network (like ShareASale or Impact) connects merchants with publishers — it's a marketplace where businesses can find affiliates to promote their products. An affiliate management platform (like Rewardful or FirstPromoter) is a SaaS tool that businesses use to run their OWN affiliate programs, manage referrals, and pay commissions directly. Many businesses use both: a management platform for direct referrals and a network for marketplace discovery.
Which affiliate platform is best for a small SaaS business starting out?
Rewardful is the best starting point for small SaaS businesses. It starts at $49/month with a 14-day free trial, has no minimum traffic requirements, integrates with Stripe and Paddle in minutes, and handles all the complex recurring commission tracking automatically. FirstPromoter is a good alternative at $47/month with similar features.
Do I need an affiliate network or can I run my own program directly?
You can absolutely run a direct affiliate program using Rewardful, FirstPromoter, or PartnerStack without joining a public network. This gives you full control over commission rates, payout terms, and affiliate relationships. Joining a network (Impact, ShareASale) adds marketplace discoverability — affiliates in the network can find your program — but comes with higher fees and less control.
Which platform has the lowest fees for affiliates?
ShareASale has the lowest barrier for affiliates — free to join, $50 minimum payout, and a huge marketplace of programs. Impact is also free for affiliates with $50 minimum. Rewardful and FirstPromoter fees are paid by the merchant, so publishers pay nothing to join their hosted programs.
Can I use multiple affiliate platforms at the same time?
Yes, many businesses run a hybrid approach: Rewardful or FirstPromoter for the core direct affiliate program, plus Impact or ShareASale for marketplace reach. Just ensure the same link doesn't get routed through two platforms — use unique tracking links per platform.
