❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which form builder has the best affiliate program?
Jotform offers 30% recurring commission with a 120-day cookie window — the longest cookie in the category. Typeform and Tally both offer 30% recurring. For passive income, any 30% recurring program on a $25-34/mo product can generate meaningful monthly revenue from 10+ referrals.
Which form builder is best for lead generation?
Typeform has the highest conversion rates due to its conversational one-question-at-a-time format. Jotform has the most templates for lead gen forms. Tally.so offers unlimited free forms and submissions, making it ideal for testing multiple lead gen funnels.
Can I accept payments through form builders?
Yes — Typeform, Jotform, Tally, Fillout, Paperform, and Cognito Forms all support Stripe integration. Jotform supports 50+ payment gateways including PayPal, Square, and Authorize.net.
Which form builder has the best free tier?
Tally.so offers the most generous free tier: unlimited forms and unlimited submissions (with some logic limitations). Fillout offers 1,000 free submissions/month on up to 3 forms. Google Forms is completely free but has the fewest features and no payment support.
Is conditional logic important in a form builder?
Yes — conditional logic lets you show/hide fields based on previous answers, creating dynamic forms that adapt to each user. This dramatically improves completion rates and data quality. All premium form builders support conditional logic.
