Frequently Asked Questions

Which digital product platform has the lowest fees?
Ko-fi Gold ($9/mo) charges 0% transaction fees. Stan Pro ($25/mo) and Podia ($39-89/mo) also charge 0% but have higher monthly fees. Payhip Plus ($29/mo) charges 2%. Gumroad charges 4.9% + $0.30 per sale but has no monthly fee.
Can I sell subscriptions on these platforms?
Yes — Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, Podia, and Stan all support recurring subscriptions/memberships. Ko-fi supports memberships through its 'Monthly Support' feature.
Which platform is best for selling templates and guides?
Gumroad has the largest audience for templates, design assets, and guides. No monthly fee makes it ideal for testing a product before committing. Payhip is a strong alternative with lower fees on the Plus plan.
Do these platforms handle EU VAT automatically?
Lemon Squeezy handles EU VAT automatically (it's registered as a merchant of record). Gumroad and Payhip collect and remit VAT for digital products. Ko-fi and Stan expect you to handle VAT compliance yourself.
Which platform has the best built-in affiliate program for creators?
Gumroad has the largest creator affiliate ecosystem where any creator can promote your product and earn commission. Payhip has a similar built-in affiliate marketplace. Lemon Squeezy offers 30% recurring affiliate commissions.
