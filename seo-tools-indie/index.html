SEO tools are expensive. Full suites from Semrush and Ahrefs cost $100-200/mo — more than most indie creators can justify before they're making real money. But there are alternatives at every price point that give you the data you actually need without the enterprise price tag. This comparison covers the spectrum from budget picks to full suites, with honest notes on where each tool falls short.

Quick Pick — Which One Should You Use?

Full SEO suite for growing sites → Semrush — Largest keyword database, most features per dollar including content marketing tools. Best for sites that need competitive analysis + keyword research + site audit in one place.
Backlink analysis & keyword research → Ahrefs — Industry standard for backlink analysis. Ahrefs Lite ($99/mo) is actually cheaper than Semrush Guru. Best if backlinks are your primary SEO focus.
Budget: low-competition keywords → LowFruits — At $29/mo, this is the most cost-effective tool for finding keywords your competitors haven't found yet.
Literally free (or nearly free) → SEO Minion — Free browser extension for on-page SEO analysis. Pro version is $49/year — less than one month of any other paid tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a paid SEO tool to start an affiliate site?
No. You can start with free tools: Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, SEO Minion for on-page, and manual SERP analysis.
Semrush vs Ahrefs — which is better?
Both are industry leaders. Semrush has a larger keyword database (25B vs 19B) and better content marketing tools. Ahrefs has the best backlink index and cleaner UX.
What's the cheapest way to get keyword research data?
Ubersuggest ($12/mo) is the cheapest standalone keyword tool. LowFruits ($29/mo) is better for finding low-competition keywords.
