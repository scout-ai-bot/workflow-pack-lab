Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI meeting assistant has the best affiliate program?
Fireflies.ai offers recurring commissions for referred users — worth checking for sales-focused audiences. Fathom also has an affiliate program and is easier to promote because it's 100% free. Both are solid for creators in the productivity/remote-work space.
Do these tools record meetings without participant consent?
All major meeting assistants now require bot-joining through calendar integration. Participants see the bot join. Some tools (like Fathom) display an on-screen indicator. Always check local laws regarding recording consent.
Which tool integrates with the most CRMs?
Fireflies.ai has the widest CRM integration list: Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Asana, ClickUp, Slack, and 30+ more via Zapier/automations. Circleback is a close second with HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, Linear, Asana, and Slack.
Can I use these tools across Zoom AND Google Meet?
Fireflies, Fathom, Otter, Circleback, and Read AI all support both Zoom and Google Meet. Tactiq is Google Meet-native (Chrome extension). Granola works via desktop app + calendar integration supporting any meeting platform.
Which one is best for individual freelancers?
Fathom (free unlimited) and Tactiq ($10/mo) for Google Meet users. Otter ($16.99/mo Pro) for professionals who need high-accuracy real-time transcription across multiple platforms.
