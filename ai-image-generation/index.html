Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI image generator has the best commercial usage rights?
Adobe Firefly has the safest commercial footing — it's trained exclusively on Adobe Stock and public domain content, meaning generated images can be used commercially without IP concerns. Canva Pro also provides full commercial rights. Midjourney offers commercial licensing on paid plans (up to $1M annual revenue). Following those, DALL-E 3 via ChatGPT Plus provides full commercial usage rights. Always verify current terms before relying on commercial usage.
Which AI image generator is best for affiliate marketers?
Canva Pro is the best affiliate play — 30% recurring commission on $13/mo Pro, plus the highest conversion volume due to Canva's massive existing user base. Adobe Firefly (via Creative Cloud referrals) can yield higher per-sale commissions but has a lower conversion rate. For content creation, Midjourney produces the highest-quality assets for marketing materials, but lacks a public affiliate program. A smart strategy: recommend Canva Pro for all-round design + affiliate earning, use Midjourney for high-end marketing visuals.
Is Stable Diffusion still relevant in 2026?
Absolutely. SD 3.5 and SDXL offer quality competitive with Midjourney for free, with full local control. The open-source ecosystem has spawned finetuned models, LoRAs, ControlNet, and IP-Adapter that enable use cases proprietary tools can't match. The trade-off: requires a decent GPU (8GB+ VRAM) or cloud compute. For the affiliate angle, SD drives traffic but has no direct affiliate program — instead, cross-link to GPU cloud hosting services and AI tool comparison pages from the SD section.
Which AI image generator handles text best in images?
Ideogram is the clear leader for text rendering in generated images — it can produce readable logos, posters, and social media graphics with accurate text. Midjourney and DALL-E 3 have improved significantly but still struggle with longer text strings. For logo design or typography-heavy graphics, Ideogram is the recommended choice. This is a key differentiator that makes Ideogram a recommended secondary tool even for users who primarily use another platform.
What's the cheapest way to get started with AI image generation?
Stable Diffusion is completely free if you have a compatible GPU (NVIDIA 1060 6GB+ or better). Adobe Firefly offers free generations (25/month on the free plan). Canva Free includes 50 AI generation credits. Leonardo AI gives 150 free generations/day. Ideogram offers 10 free daily credits. For maximum free usage without limits, run Stable Diffusion locally with Automatic1111 or ComfyUI web interfaces.
