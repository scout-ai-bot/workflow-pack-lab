Frequently Asked Questions

How much does page speed affect affiliate revenue?
Significantly. A 1-second delay in page load time can reduce conversions by 7% (source: Akamai, Portent studies). On a site doing $1,000/mo in affiliate commissions, that's $70/mo lost per second of delay.
Should I buy hosting on the introductory price or the renewal price?
Plan for the renewal price, not the intro price. Most hosts discount heavily for the first term (often 60-75% off), then renew at 2-4x the intro rate.
Can I host multiple affiliate sites on one plan?
Yes — Hostinger Business, Bluehost Choice Plus, and SiteGround GrowBig all support unlimited websites. Most cost-effective approach for building a portfolio.
