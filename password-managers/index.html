❓ FAQ

Is it safe to store passwords in a password manager?
Yes — all managers listed use zero-knowledge encryption (your master password is never stored on servers). Independent security audits are published annually by each provider.
Which password manager has the best affiliate program?
NordPass offers 75% on new monthly subscriptions, 30% on other plans, 10% on renewals (per nordpass.com/affiliate, checked 2026-06-11). 1Password offers terms published on its official partner page — verify before relying on a rate. Both are popular programs, but rates vary by plan and region.
Can I migrate from one password manager to another?
Yes. All major managers support CSV import/export. 1Password and Bitwarden offer direct import from most competitors.
