📐 Methodology

Prices are sourced from each platform's public pricing page as of June 2026. Domain costs are estimated at $12-15/yr (.com). SSL is included free on all platforms listed. "Extras" include required premium features (e-commerce transaction fees, extra storage, custom code export) based on site type. Total cost = plan subscription × years + domain × years + extras. Annual billing typically saves 20-40% vs monthly. Promotional first-term pricing is shown but renewal pricing may differ — we note both where known.

Why this matters: Most comparisons only show starting price. The real cost difference between a "cheap" builder and a "premium" one can be 5-10x over 3-5 years — and the cheapest option often lacks the features you need.
