Selling to an existing audience (newsletter, social) → Gumroad — Best brand recognition, simplest checkout flow, built-in affiliate system.

Zero risk / testing the waters → Ko-fi — No monthly fee. Good for hobbyists and testing product ideas with minimal commitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platform has the lowest fees?
Ko-fi has 0% transaction fees (with $9/mo Gold membership). Payhip Plus ($29/mo) charges 2% per sale. Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy both charge 5% per sale plus payment processing fees.
Which platform is best for affiliate marketing?
Gumroad has the most mature built-in affiliate system, letting you offer up to 50% commission to affiliates. Lemon Squeezy and Payhip also have built-in affiliate systems. Ko-fi does not currently support affiliate programs.
Can I use multiple platforms at once?
Yes, many creators list on Gumroad and cross-link to their Ko-fi shop or Payhip store. There are no exclusivity requirements on any of these platforms.
Which platform handles EU VAT automatically?
Lemon Squeezy is the only one that handles EU VAT compliance automatically — they remit VAT on your behalf. On other platforms, you may need to handle VAT yourself depending on your jurisdiction.
