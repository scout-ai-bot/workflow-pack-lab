Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI writing tool generates the best blog content?
For affiliate content specifically, RightBlogger has the best template system purpose-built for SEO blog posts. For raw content quality, Claude is generally considered the best for long-form, thoughtful writing. For volume and speed, Writesonic's Chatsonic mode is very efficient.
Do AI writing tools have affiliate programs?
Yes, RightBlogger (50% lifetime recurring), Writesonic (25-30% lifetime), and Jasper (30% recurring) all have strong affiliate programs. Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) do NOT have affiliate programs.
How much AI-written content is OK for Google rankings?
Google's guidance says AI content is fine as long as it's helpful, original, and demonstrates E-E-A-T. Heavily AI-generated content without human editing and fact-checking typically performs worse. Best practice: use AI for drafts and outlines, then heavily edit for accuracy, voice, and unique insights.
