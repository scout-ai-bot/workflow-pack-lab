❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What CRM is best for a solopreneur on a budget?
Both HubSpot and Freshsales offer genuinely useful free tiers that handle basic contact management, deal tracking, and pipeline management. HubSpot's free tier is more feature-rich (meetings, tasks, live chat) but pushes upgrades aggressively. Freshsales' free tier is simpler but effective for solo operators. For paid plans, Freshsales at $9/seat/mo or Pipedrive at $14.90/mo are the most affordable entry points.
Which CRM pays the highest affiliate commission?
HubSpot offers the highest potential payout — up to 100% of first month on paid plans. A single Starter plan referral ($20/mo) earns $20, but an Enterprise referral ($1,200+/mo) can earn $1,200+. For consistent recurring income, Close offers 30% lifetime recurring (a 5-seat team at $25/seat/mo = $37.50/mo in perpetuity). Pipedrive offers 20% recurring with 4-month enterprise bonuses.
Do I need a CRM as a solopreneur?
If you manage more than 20-30 contacts, have a sales pipeline with stages, or send follow-ups manually, a CRM will pay for itself in time saved and deals not lost. HubSpot's free tier is ideal to start with zero commitment. As your deal flow grows, upgrading to Pipedrive or Close adds pipeline visibility and automation that directly increases close rates.
What's the difference between a CRM and project management software?
CRM is designed for managing relationships and sales pipelines (contacts, deals, communications). Project management tools (Asana, Trello, Monday.com) are designed for task tracking and team workflows. Monday.com and Notion blur the line by offering both CRM and project management modules. For affiliate purposes, CRM generally has higher per-seat pricing and lower churn, making it a more valuable commission stream.
Can I migrate from a free CRM to a paid one easily?
Yes — most platforms make this seamless. HubSpot preserves all your contacts, deals, and pipelines when you upgrade. Pipedrive imports from CSV, HubSpot, Salesforce, and other CRMs directly. Freshsales offers one-click migration from HubSpot. This low switching friction means users are more likely to start free and convert, which benefits affiliate marketers with recurring commissions.
