Frequently Asked Questions

Which platform has the best affiliate program for its own users?
beehiiv pays up to 60% recurring — the highest standard rate. Kit (ConvertKit) pays 50% first 12 months + 30% recurring. Both are excellent but beehiiv's lifetime recurring on a platform with built-in ads gives it the edge for long-term passive income.
Can I migrate from one platform to another?
Yes, all major email platforms allow subscriber import/export. Migration complexity varies: Mailchimp to beehiiv or Kit is straightforward. Substack to beehiiv is also well-documented. Expect some list churn (5-15%) after migration.
Do any platforms offer an ad marketplace for newsletter monetization?
beehiiv's Boost Network is the only native ad marketplace among these platforms. It connects newsletter creators with advertisers and handles ad placement and payment — a significant differentiator for monetization beyond subscriptions.
