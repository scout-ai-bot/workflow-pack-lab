Frequently Asked Questions

Which web host pays the highest affiliate commission per sale?
WP Engine and DreamHost both offer up to $200 per referred sale. Kinsta can exceed $500 per referral on enterprise plans. However, Hostinger's 60% commission structure (approximately $125 on the average Business plan sale) has a higher conversion rate due to the lower price point. For recurring revenue, Cloudways pays on subscription value while WP Engine's $200 is a flat fee per sale.
Which host has the best cookie duration for affiliates?
Hostinger leads with 120 days (4 months). WP Engine, DreamHost, and A2 Hosting offer 90-day cookies. SiteGround and Cloudways use 60-day cookies. Bluehost and Kinsta use 30-day cookies. For context: a 120-day cookie means if someone clicks your link today and buys up to 4 months later, you still get credit — significantly better for content that ranks on long-tail SEO queries where purchase intent may take weeks to materialize.
What's the difference between shared, VPS, and managed WordPress hosting?
Shared hosting (Hostinger, Bluehost, SiteGround entry plans) — multiple sites share one server. Cheapest but limited resources. VPS (Cloudways, A2 VPS, DreamHost VPS) — dedicated virtual resources, scalable, more control. Managed WordPress (WP Engine, Kinsta, SiteGround GrowBig+) — WP-optimized, automatic updates, staging, premium support. Affiliate commissions tend to be higher per-sale on managed WordPress plans ($100-$500+) due to higher price points.
Which web host is best for a beginner affiliate site?
Hostinger's Business plan ($3.99/mo) is ideal — supports 100 websites, includes free domain + SSL, and the 120-day affiliate cookie gives you maximum attribution time. Bluehost's Basic plan ($2.95/mo) is the most beginner-friendly with WordPress.org's official recommendation. For a site that plans to scale, SiteGround's GrowBig plan ($5.99/mo) offers the best balance of performance, support, and growth capacity.
Do these hosting providers offer student or non-profit discounts?
DreamHost offers verified non-profit discounts. SiteGround has occasional educational institution pricing. Most shared hosting providers (Hostinger, Bluehost) are already priced low enough ($2.69-$2.95/mo) that dedicated student plans aren't needed. For the affiliate angle: students and non-profits are typically lower-converting audiences for premium plans — focus on freelancers, small business owners, and bloggers for the highest conversion rates.
