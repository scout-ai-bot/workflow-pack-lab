Frequently Asked Questions

Which newsletter platform pays the highest affiliate commission?
beehiiv pays up to 60% recurring commission through its Rewardful partnership. This is the highest rate among major newsletter platforms. Kit (ConvertKit) pays 50% recurring for the first 12 months.
Can I run ads in my newsletter alongside paid subscriptions?
Yes, with beehiiv (Boost Network) and Kit (Creator Network). beehiiv's Boost Network is the most mature advertising marketplace, allowing you to monetize both free and paid subscribers through sponsored content.
Which platform has the most generous free tier?
beehiiv offers up to 2,500 subscribers on its free plan — more than double Kit and ConvertKit's 1,000 subscriber limits. Substack doesn't limit subscriber count but takes 10% of revenue.
Do I need a custom domain for a paid newsletter?
Most platforms let you start with a subdomain (yourname.beehiiv.com, yourname.substack.com). A custom domain is recommended for brand consistency and can be added at any time. Ghost requires a custom domain.
Which platform is best for a beginner creating their first paid newsletter?
beehiiv offers the best balance of free tier (2,500 subscribers), built-in growth tools (Boost ads, referral programs), and monetization (paid subscriptions, affiliate commisions). Start on beehiiv, graduate to Ghost if you need full ownership later.
