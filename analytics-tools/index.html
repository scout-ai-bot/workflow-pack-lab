Detailed Breakdown

Google Analytics 4

Price: Free (standard) / $50K+/yr (GA360 enterprise)

Best for: Enterprises and sites that need the full Google ecosystem (Ads, Search Console, BigQuery integration). GA4 is free at scale — unlimited pageviews, but requires GDPR cookie consent in Europe.

Privacy: Must self-configure consent mode and data deletion. Google's data-sharing terms remain a concern for privacy-focused sites. No cookie-free tracking option.

Cookie duration: 2 years (configurable) · Data retention: 2-14 months (user-level; aggregated forever)

Verdict: Use if you need GA360 or deep Google Ads integration. For most indie sites, the privacy-lightweight alternatives below are better.

FAQ

Do I need a cookie banner with Plausible or Fathom?

No. Both use cookie-free tracking — no personal data is collected, so GDPR cookie consent is not required. Legal teams generally confirm this, but always check with your own counsel.

Which analytics tool is best for an affiliate site?

Plausible or Fathom. Both are lightweight (fast page loads = better SEO), privacy-compliant, and cheap. The 30% recurring affiliate commissions also let you earn while you track.

Can I import my Google Analytics data?

Plausible, Fathom, and Matomo offer CSV-based imports. Umami does not have an import tool. Historical data is rarely critical — most sites start fresh with a clean privacy-first approach.

Which platforms have self-hosted options?

Umami (free, MIT license), Matomo (free, GPLv3), Fathom (paid self-hosted license), and Plausible (enterprise only). Self-hosting gives full data control but requires server maintenance.
